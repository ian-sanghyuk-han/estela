# What already exists

Surveyed 2026-08-24, because the owner asked whether anyone had built this. Someone has —
several people — and it is better to know precisely what they do than to rediscover it.

---

## Beli — the closest thing to the ranking half

A restaurant app, strongest in the United States and especially New York.

**What it does that we now also do:** when you add a restaurant it asks you to compare it
head-to-head with ones you already ranked, until it can place it. It asks *"was it better
than that one?"* rather than *"how good was it?"* — a forced order with no ties. It keeps
Been and Want-to-Try, friends whose lists you can read, and a **friend rating** averaging
your friends' scores for a place.

**Taste Profile** is the piece worth stealing outright: a summary of how you have ranked
by cuisine, city and country — total eaten, top five cuisines, the city you eat in most.
It turns a log into a portrait.

**What users complain about, in their own words:**

- Onboarding will not let you past **inviting four friends**.
- The average-score feature **only unlocks if you refer someone** — users call it an
  "MLM vibe" and say it makes them awkward about recommending the app.
- **Missing restaurants**, badly in mainland China, and locations frequently wrong.
- No restaurant information beyond the venue's own website, which often does not exist.
- **Streaks** become the point — people post to keep a streak rather than out of interest.
- Skews to expensive restaurants and manufactures "unmerited buzz".

## Polarsteps — the closest thing to the trail

20 million travellers as of March 2026. Records a trip **by GPS as you go** and draws the
route on a map, with photos and notes per stop. Structurally the same gesture as our line
between stamps, except it traces travel rather than meals, and there is nothing to complete.

## The completion trackers

**Been** (countries, yes/no), **Visited** (tap countries, heat map, percent of world).
Denominator-and-completion products with no places inside them.

## Where that leaves us

Three of Beli's six complaints are things we already answer, because of the registry work:

| Their problem | Our position |
|---|---|
| Missing restaurants | 368,951 government-registered places across nine jurisdictions, plus 정선's 순대국집 |
| Locations wrong | Coordinates come from the state, or from the state's own geocoder |
| No info beyond a website | Cuisine, founding year, award year, category, address, website |

Two more we simply decline to do: **nothing is gated behind an invite**, and there are no
streaks. A list has to work alone, on the first day, with no friends.

The sixth — skewing to expensive places — is a live risk for us and worse, since our canon
layers are Michelin and UNESCO. **내 목록 is the counterweight:** a 순대국집 must be able to
stand at the top of a list with the same weight as a three-star kitchen.

**And the real difference is not the ranking.** Beli knows where your friends went. We know
what exists and how far through it you are. So the sentence that is ours is not
*"내 이탈리안 TOP 4"* but *"프랑스 미슐랭 31곳 중 3곳 · 내 1위는 Guy Savoy"*.
